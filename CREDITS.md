## Item icons

The glyphs on the item and power-up drops are from
[game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), licensed under
[Creative Commons BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The unmodified SVGs are in `docs/reference/items/`. `pipeline/item_icons.py`
recolours them and sets them into painted medallions; that change is ours.
